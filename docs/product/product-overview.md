# Product overview

Status: Current

Kingshot Alliance is a coordination platform for KingShot communities. It supports account security, Player identity/context, Alliance management, Kingdom governance, Events and execution planning, intelligence/reporting, communications delivery and cross-tenant platform administration.

## Primary actors

- **User** — account holder who authenticates to the application.
- **Player** — a KingShot persona owned/claimed by a User and used as the game-domain actor.
- **Alliance member/leader** — a Player with Alliance membership, rank and optional specialist roles.
- **Kingdom leader/role holder** — a Player with Kingdom governance authority.
- **Coordinator** — a Player authorized for Event/Operations capabilities.
- **Platform Administrator** — a User with cross-tenant SaaS administration; not an in-game bypass.

## Core product areas

1. secure account access and MFA;
2. multiple Player personas with active Player selection;
3. Alliance lifecycle, membership, roles, recruitment and content;
4. Kingdom identity/governance and transfer-related coordination;
5. Events, participation, rosters, battle planning, rallies, results and King Perks;
6. roster/contribution/event/diplomacy intelligence and sharing;
7. a Governor command overview, scheduled Alliance announcement broadcasts, and reminders/notifications with retryable in-app, Discord and Telegram delivery;
8. platform lifecycle, event-type administration, API credentials and webhooks;
9. composed dashboards/history/settings views across business contexts.

The product deliberately separates current game authority from historical observations and platform administration.
